How to Use
Toggle High Accuracy Mode

Use the toggle switch at the top to enable or disable high accuracy mode. When enabled, only recognitions meeting the 90% confidence threshold will be accepted.

Camera Setup

Ensure good lighting, position your face in the center of the frame, and keep the camera steady for best results.

Monitor Quality

Watch the real-time quality indicators to see confidence, quality score, and system status. Adjust your position if needed.

Security Features

The system includes rate limiting, cooldown periods, and multi-verification to ensure security and prevent abuse.
